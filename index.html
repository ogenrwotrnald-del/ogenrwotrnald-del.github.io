Biography

Ronald Ogenrwot is a Research Fellow and Quantitative Economist at Makerere University specializing in Applied Econometrics, Forced Migration Economics, and Spatial Environmental Sustainability. His research utilizes advanced computational methods to analyze structural system dynamics. Mr. Ogenrwot is an alumnus of both the Volkswagen Foundation SNRM Network and the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS).
