Biography

I am a Research Fellow and Quantitative Economist at Makerere University (College of Business and Management Sciences). My work bridges the gap between complex econometric mathematics and real-world policy impact, specializing in Applied Econometrics, Forced Migration Economics, and Spatial Environmental Sustainability.

As a competitive fellowship winner and alumnus of the Volkswagen Foundation SNRM Network and the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS), I deploy advanced computational data pipelines (R, Python, PostgreSQL, Google Earth Engine) to stress-test and predict structural system collapses across East Africa.
